# Shops & Establishments / Employment — Selected Provisions (paraphrased for demo use)

## Notice period and termination
State Shops & Establishments Acts generally require a minimum notice period
(commonly 30 days, varies by state) or pay in lieu, before terminating an
employee without cause. Termination for proven misconduct after due enquiry is
typically exempt from the standard notice requirement.

## Probation
Employment contracts may specify a probation period, but the maximum
permissible probation duration and any extension rules vary by state-specific
Shops & Establishments legislation.

## Working hours and overtime
Most state Acts cap standard working hours (commonly 9 hours/day, 48
hours/week) and require overtime compensation, typically at twice the ordinary
rate, for hours worked beyond the cap.
