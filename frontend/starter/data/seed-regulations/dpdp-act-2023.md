# Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — Selected Provisions (paraphrased for demo use)

## Consent
Personal data may generally only be processed with the data principal's
consent, given after a clear notice describing the personal data being
collected and the purpose of processing. Consent must be freely given,
specific, informed, and unconditional.

## Data retention and erasure
Data fiduciaries must erase personal data once the specified purpose is no
longer being served and retention is not required by law, unless a longer
retention period is otherwise permitted for the relevant class of fiduciary.

## Significant Data Fiduciaries
Entities notified as "Significant Data Fiduciaries" (based on volume/
sensitivity of data processed) face additional obligations: appointing a Data
Protection Officer based in India, conducting periodic Data Protection Impact
Assessments, and independent audits.
