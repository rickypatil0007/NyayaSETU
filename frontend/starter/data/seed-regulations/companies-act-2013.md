# Companies Act, 2013 — Selected Sections (paraphrased for demo use)

## Section 134 — Financial Statement, Board's Report
Every company must prepare its financial statements and Board's report for each
financial year, and the Board's report must include specified disclosures
(e.g. director responsibility statement, related-party transaction details).

## Section 188 — Related Party Transactions
Contracts or arrangements with related parties (as defined) require Board
approval, and beyond prescribed thresholds, shareholder approval by resolution.
Certain transactions in the ordinary course of business at arm's length are
exempt from the approval requirement.

## Section 203 — Appointment of Key Managerial Personnel
Certain classes of companies (based on paid-up share capital thresholds) are
required to appoint whole-time key managerial personnel (MD/CEO, CS, CFO).
Smaller companies below the threshold are not required to make these
appointments.
