# PART I
## CHAPTER I
### Rule 1. Short title and commencement.
(1) These rules may be called the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette, in phases as notified by the Data Protection Board.

## CHAPTER II: NOTICE AND CONSENT
### Rule 4. Form of Notice.
(1) The notice given by a Data Fiduciary to a Data Principal shall clearly state:
(a) the personal data to be processed;
(b) the purpose for which the personal data is to be processed;
(c) the manner in which the Data Principal may exercise her rights under the Act.
(2) The notice shall be provided in English and in the 22 languages specified in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution.

## CHAPTER IV: SIGNIFICANT DATA FIDUCIARIES
### Rule 10. Data Protection Impact Assessment.
(1) A Significant Data Fiduciary shall conduct a Data Protection Impact Assessment prior to undertaking any processing of personal data which involves new technologies or carries a high risk to the rights of Data Principals.
