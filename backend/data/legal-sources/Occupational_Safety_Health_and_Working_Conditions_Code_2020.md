# PART I
## CHAPTER I
### Section 1. Short title, extent and commencement.
(1) This Act may be called the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020.
(2) It extends to the whole of India.

## CHAPTER II: REGISTRATION
### Section 3. Registration of certain establishments.
Every employer of an establishment which comes into existence after the commencement of this Code and to which this Code applies shall, within sixty days from the date of such applicability, apply for registration of the establishment.

## CHAPTER IV: OCCUPATIONAL SAFETY AND HEALTH
### Section 18. Duties of employer.
(1) Every employer shall ensure that the workplace is free from hazards which cause or are likely to cause injury or occupational disease to the employees.
