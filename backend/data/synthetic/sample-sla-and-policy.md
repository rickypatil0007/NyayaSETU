## Part A — AcmeCloud Service Agreement (synthetic)

### 1. Service Availability
Provider will target 99.5% monthly availability. Scheduled maintenance announced at least 24 hours in advance is excluded from availability calculations.

### 2. Service Credits
If monthly availability falls below 99.5%, Customer may request a service credit equal to 5% of the monthly fee. The credit is Customer's sole remedy for any service interruption, regardless of duration or business impact.

### 3. Auto-Renewal
The Agreement automatically renews for successive twelve-month terms unless Customer gives written notice at least 90 days before the current term ends.

### 4. Limitation of Liability
Customer's aggregate liability is limited to fees paid during the three months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Provider's aggregate liability is not subject to a monetary cap.

### 5. Indemnity
Customer will defend and indemnify Provider and its affiliates against all third-party claims arising from Customer's use of the Service. Provider has no reciprocal indemnification obligation.

### 6. Confidentiality
Each party will protect the other party's Confidential Information during the term. The obligations in this section end when the Agreement terminates.

### 7. Termination
Provider may terminate immediately for material breach. Customer may terminate for material breach only after giving 30 days' written notice and an opportunity to cure.

## Part B — Startup Procurement Policy (synthetic)

### P1. Liability
New SaaS vendors should have a mutual aggregate liability cap no greater than twelve months of fees unless Legal approves an exception.

### P2. Indemnity
Indemnity obligations should be mutual for third-party intellectual-property claims and must be reviewed when obligations apply only to the customer.

### P3. Renewal
Automatic renewal notice periods over 60 days require an owner reminder and procurement approval.

### P4. Confidentiality
Confidentiality obligations should survive termination for at least two years unless a documented exception is approved.

### P5. SLA Remedy
For a business-critical service, an agreement that limits all interruption remedies to a small service credit requires operational review.
